Methodology

Approach

This analysis employed Gioia Methodology (Gioia, Corley & Hamilton, 2013), a systematic, rigorous approach to grounded-theory-style qualitative research designed to meet standards of scholarly rigour while remaining close to participant data.

Three-Tier Coding Structure
  1. First-order concepts — Each transcript segment coded using the informant's own language; labels stay close to the data with verbatim excerpts. Generated by claude-haiku-4-5 with Gioia-specific prompting (2–4 concepts per segment).
  2. Second-order themes — Theoretical categories derived by clustering first-order concept labels using claude-opus-4-7 (adaptive thinking). Written in researcher/theoretical language, more abstract than 1st-order labels.
  3. Aggregate dimensions — Overarching theoretical constructs grouping 2nd-order themes; represent the core theoretical contribution of the analysis. Rich descriptions synthesised by claude-opus-4-7 in academic register.
